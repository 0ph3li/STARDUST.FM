    02 · the small print, in big print
    
      
      
        
    1. 01
      there is no server.
      STARDUST.FM is a folder of html, css and javascript. There's no database, no login and no backend: nothing you type is sent anywhere. Your wishes, your sign, your dedications and your posters stay in this browser.
      2. 
        
    2. 02
      no cookies, no analytics, no ads.
      Nothing tracks you, counts you or follows you to other sites. The only storage used is your browser's local storage (things kept until you clear them) and session storage (forgotten when you close the tab).
      3. 
        
    3. 03
      what other services see.
      To look like this, the pages load fonts from Google Fonts and two animation libraries (GSAP, Lenis) from public CDNs (cdnjs, jsDelivr). Like any website, those services receive your IP address and browser details when the files are requested. Nothing you do on the site is sent with them.
      4. 
        
    4. 04
      the radio is maths, not a microphone.
      Every song is synthesised live on your device with the Web Audio API. The site never asks for your microphone or camera, and audio only starts when you press play.
      5. 
        
    5. 05
      your birthday is never saved.
      On the horoscope page your birthday is only used, right there, to pick a sign. The sign is remembered; the date isn't.
      6. 
        
    6. 06
      your own pictures stay in the tab.
      A picture you add in the poster studio is read by your browser only. It's never uploaded and it isn't kept after you close the tab. Downloaded posters and calendar reminders are made in your browser and saved to your device like any download.
      7. 
        
    7. 07
      how to forget.
      Let single stars fall in the memory sky above, hold the button below to forget everything, type stardust.forget() in the console, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. All three do the same thing.
